======

PI: Dennis McGillicuddy. Funded by NOAA GOMO, NOAA OAP, and NSF. Contribution: model development, data analysis, and publication writing.
* **U.S. National Science Foundation, OCE-2343008** (2024-2025): Toward a more complete understanding of coastal upwelling dynamics. PI: Xiaozhou Ruan. Contribution: model implementation, data analysis, and publication writing.

PI: Jianping Gan. Contribution: model development, data analysis, and publication writing.
